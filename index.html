Checkpoint 1

Handle made hands first

Handle both groups before using the main ladder: unbreakable hands on top, then breakable made hands below.

UNBREAKABLE Never break these

Straight flush
Hold all five cards.
Four of a kind
Hold the four matching cards. Keeping the kicker is EV-equivalent, but drawing one is standard.
Full house
Hold all five cards.
Three of a kind
Hold the trips; discard both kickers.
Two pair
Hold both pairs; discard the fifth card.

BREAKABLE Usually hold, except…

Straight
Hold all five. ↳1only for four to a royal. An ordinary four to a straight flush does not break a straight.
Flush
Hold all five. ↳1only for four to a royal.
High pair
Hold Jacks or better. ↳1for four to a royal; ↳2for four to a straight flush.

Non-made-hand ladder

The five purple rungs use one compact statistic, so every non-royal three-card straight-flush draw (3SF) fits directly into the main ladder.
3SF score
Score = H + S
H = held high cards (J, Q, K, A). S = distinct five-card straights containing the three suited ranks. Three to a royal is classified separately.
